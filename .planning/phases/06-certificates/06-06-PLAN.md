## Worker decisions (planner, recorded for the SUMMARY)

| Decision | Reason | Alternative not taken |
|---|---|---|
| One coordinator-level timer at the earliest floor-day change + 1 s | One handle to cancel; the +1 s lands after the floor value changed, so a fire never computes the old value; covers threshold, expiry and the daily countdown with one rule | Per-entity timers; firing at the exact boundary (computes the unchanged value) |
| The timer is scheduled from the end of a successful `_async_update_data` | No self-listener (would change listener counts and HA's interval logic) | Override `async_set_updated_data` |
| Retry after a failed cert cycle in 5 minutes (`CERT_RETRY_INTERVAL = 300`), back to 6 h after success | A transient API failure at a 6-hourly cycle would otherwise leave certificate entities unavailable for 6 h while the rest of the integration recovers in seconds | Keep the 6 h interval |
| After a failed first cycle, registry orphans without a stored missing time are adopted as present; stored times are kept | A failed cycle carries no information about which hosts exist (same rule as `_async_kind_updated`); stored times describe a state from before the restart | Defer kind registration until the first success |
