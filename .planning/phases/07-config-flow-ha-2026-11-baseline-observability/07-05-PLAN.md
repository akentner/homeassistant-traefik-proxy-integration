## Scope justification (task and file count)

Three tasks (`verify.plan-structure` task_count 3; the checker's "4" counted the `<tasks>` wrapper), 12 files. Task 1 is the atomic entry-shape switch: the reader (`auth_from_entry_data`), every writer (user, reauth, reconfigure) and the `< 3` migration must change in one commit, otherwise a new entry stored at minor version 3 with legacy data, or a 1.3 entry read by the legacy reader, breaks setup; that coupling plus the test adaptations is the 12-file surface, and Tasks 2-3 add no new file. A plan split into "migration" and "flow/selector" therefore cannot keep every commit green without interim writer code that the next plan deletes. The two one-way changes (data shape 1.3, unique_id `host:port`) share the single `< 3` migration block and its tests, are both settled by the user (M15, M21, W44, D-14), and reach users only together in the Phase 8 release; Task 3 keeps the unique_id change in its own commit.
