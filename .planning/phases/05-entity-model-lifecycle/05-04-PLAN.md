<objective>
The per-item option lets large installs keep only aggregate, diagnostic and certificate entities: with it off, no router or service entity and no Routers/Services sub-device exists, and turning it off removes the existing ones at the next entry reload.

Purpose: ENT-10 (D-06, the per-item part of D-21). Certificate entities move onto `TraefikCertEntity` and the manager in 05-05.
